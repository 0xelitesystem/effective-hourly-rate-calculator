# Effective Hourly Rate Calculator

Your effective hourly rate is your income after expenses divided by every hour you actually work, including the unpaid ones. This tool computes it from your income, your billable and non-billable hours, and your business expenses, then shows the gap between that real rate and the nominal rate you think you charge.

**Live demo:** https://0xelitesystem.github.io/effective-hourly-rate-calculator/

## What it does

A nominal rate divides gross income by billable hours alone. The effective rate divides income after expenses by total hours worked: billable, admin, sales, learning, and overhead. The difference is the time and cost that quietly lower what an hour of your work pays.

Enter income for a period, the hours of each kind for the same period, and your expenses. The tool returns the effective hourly rate, the nominal rate for comparison, total hours worked, income after expenses, and the per-hour gap between nominal and effective.

## Aesthetic

A buff time-clock punch card: perforated edges, monospace figures, a red serial stamp, and an overdue-style verdict on how wide the gap runs.

## Not financial advice

This tool performs arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It is not financial, tax, accounting, or legal advice, and it does not account for your specific circumstances. Verify any figure before acting on it.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, stored, or saved. Closing the tab clears it.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or host it as a static page. No build step, no dependencies, no network calls.
